The mark keeps its dark tile on light ground

The mark is the app icon distilled: two ribbons rising and crossing, violet at the top edge, teal at the bottom, on a tile one step above the canvas (#151A24) so it still reads as an icon against #0B0D12. The same two curves, blurred hard and dropped to low opacity, become the ambient field behind the hero — one motif doing two jobs. Never recolour the ribbons, never set the wordmark in anything but Outfit, and never put the gradient wordmark over an image.
