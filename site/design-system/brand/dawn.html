Dawn — identity proposal

One mark, two readings: a sun cresting the horizon, and a play triangle cut out of it. Dawn is what Aurora means in Latin, so nothing about the idea changes — the palette just turns the corner from an aurora's cold violet-teal to a sunrise's violet-rose-amber.

It has to survive 16px

Three elements is the whole mark. An outer "first light" arc and reflection dashes below the horizon were both drawn and then cut: at 38px they closed the gaps and the thing read as a blob. The app icon keeps the dashes because at 1024px there is room for them.

Rules. The play triangle is negative space — cut from the sun in the tile colour, never drawn as a separate shape, or it stops reading as one object. Never rotate the mark: the horizon is a horizon. Never recolour the gradient, and never run it top-to-bottom on the wordmark — it goes left to right, the way light arrives. The sun never sits below its horizon.

Why this works as a rename. Dawn means what Aurora means, so the ribbon motif's job — one light phenomenon, violet through to warm — carries over intact. The app's #7C5CFF accent survives as "first light", which means the existing UI does not have to be repainted to adopt this.
