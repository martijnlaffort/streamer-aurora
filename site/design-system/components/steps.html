How-it-works steps

Three steps, because there are three. The numerals carry the gradient so the section reads as a sequence at a glance, and the hairline track behind them does the arrow's job without an arrow.

01

Point it at your line

An Xtream Codes server URL with your username and password, or an M3U playlist plus an optional XMLTV guide URL. Aurora checks the credentials before it saves them, and the password goes to the device keychain — never into its database.

02

It takes the catalogue in

Films, series and channels are cached locally and indexed, then refreshed a category at a time as you browse rather than in one sweep. A line with a hundred thousand titles stays scrollable, and what you already loaded still opens with the network off.

03

Then it keeps up with you

Resume points, language choices and My List are yours from the first minute. Turn sync on and a second device picks up the same positions.

Pairing one is a code, not a re-typed password: Settings → Pair a device.

Rules. Steps describe what happens, not what to click. Each one is at most three sentences; the keyboard-style chip is the only place a literal UI path appears, and only in the last step.
