Elevation

Flat + hairlineThe default. On a near-black canvas a 1px white-7% border separates surfaces better than any shadow.

--shadow-liftDevice mockups only. 0 28px 70px −24px black/85 — the frame has to sit above the page, not float.

--glow-violetThe one accent elevation: 0 18px 50px −18px violet/55. Hero device and nothing else.

Layout. Content maxes at 1120px with 24px gutters; the hero is allowed to run full-bleed because the ambient ribbons need the room. Breakpoints are 720px (stack everything) and 1040px (two columns become three). No shadow on a card, ever — that is the one rule that keeps the site from looking like a light-theme layout painted dark.
