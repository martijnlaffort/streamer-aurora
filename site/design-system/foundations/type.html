Typography

Two faces, as in the app (PRD §10): Outfit for anything titular, Inter for everything you actually read. Web-only additions are the hero size and the 19px lead — the app has no equivalent because a phone never needs one. If Google Fonts is unavailable the stack falls back to the system UI face, and nothing about the layout moves.

It remembers where you stopped

Not roughly. Not the start of the episode. The second you walked away — across app restarts, and across devices if you turn sync on.

Rules. One hero per page. Eyebrows are always teal, always uppercase, and never longer than four words. Measure caps at 68ch for body, 56ch for lead. Numerals are tabular in the spec strip (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums) and nowhere else.
