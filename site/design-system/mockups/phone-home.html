Every element here exists in the app: the rotating hero with its Details and My List buttons and page dots, Continue Watching with a violet progress bar and the episode it will resume, a numbered Top 10, and the six-tab bar.

  • The hero cross-fades every six seconds and stops as soon as you engage with it — a title that changes under your thumb is a hazard, not a feature.
  • The third Top 10 poster carries the white focus ring. It is drawn in a phone mockup on purpose: the same component takes a D-pad on the television.
  • Continue Watching says S2 · E4, not just the show name — finishing an episode advances the row instead of dropping the series.
  • Artwork is deliberately abstract and the titles are invented. The site never shows a real catalogue, which is both a legal and a privacy decision.
