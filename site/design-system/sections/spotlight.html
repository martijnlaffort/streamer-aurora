The player

Two rows of chrome, then it gets out of the way

Everything you need is one tap deep and always in the same place. The two buttons in the top-right are audio and subtitles — the pair this app was built to get right.

  • Track lists show the language of every track, embedded or external, with an explicit Off for subtitles.
  • Skip ten seconds, drag to seek, or swipe for brightness and volume without a menu.
  • Up next counts down between episodes, and you can take it or leave it.
  • Lock the controls so a pocket can't seek, and keep the audio playing with the screen off.

Live TV & the big screen

It grew a television

The same components, with focus turned up: a navigation rail instead of a tab bar, and a white ring wherever the D-pad is. Nothing was forked to get there.

  • A day-bounded guide with now and next on every channel, from your provider's XMLTV feed.
  • Missed the first four minutes? Watch from the start, where the provider offers catch-up.
  • Channel up and down from inside the stream — zapping, not back-out-and-pick-again.
  • Adding the TV is a pairing code, not your provider password typed with a remote.
