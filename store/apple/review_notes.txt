WHAT DAWN PLAYER IS

Dawn Player is a general-purpose video player for the user's own media. It ships with no content of any kind and has no catalogue, store, account or backend of its own. Out of the box it plays video files on the device; it can also play a playlist the user adds, or sign in to a media server the user already has an account with.

WHAT IS DIFFERENT IN THIS BUILD

- Video files on the device are now the first way in. The first screen offers, in this order: "Open a video file", "Add a playlist link or file", "Connect a media server". Dawn Player is registered for common video types (MP4, MOV, M4V, MKV, WebM, AVI, MPEG-TS) so a video can be opened from the Files app or another app's share sheet, in place, without copying.
- Resume survives restarts, including for files opened from Files: the app keeps a security-scoped bookmark, so the same file reopens at the same second.
- The audio and subtitle language the user picks is remembered and selected automatically on every later video that has it. External .srt/.ass subtitle files can be loaded for a video and are remembered for it.
- Playback is libmpv (the open-source mpv engine), not AVPlayer, so it plays MKV, HEVC, MPEG-TS and multi-audio HLS that the system player cannot.
- The Live tab no longer appears unless a source the user added actually contains live streams. Nothing in the app suggests, links to or helps find content.

HOW TO REVIEW (no login needed)

1. Launch the app.
2. Tap "Add a playlist link or file".
3. Paste this link into "Playlist link" and tap Connect:
   https://dawnplayer.com/demo/demo.m3u
4. Tap "Start watching", then the Movies tab. The playlist contains three items, all hosted by us and all free to redistribute:
   - Big Buck Bunny (2008), Blender Foundation, CC BY 3.0
   - Sintel (2010), Blender Foundation, CC BY 3.0
   - Apple's HLS sample stream (bipbop), from Apple's developer documentation; it has several subtitle languages and audio tracks
5. Play a film, leave it part-way, close the app and reopen it: it is in Continue Watching and resumes at the same position.
6. In the HLS sample, open Audio & subtitles and choose French subtitles. Open it again: French is selected automatically.

To try a local file: save any video to Files, then use the share button in Files and choose Dawn Player, or tap the "Open a video file" icon at the top of Home.

The "Connect a media server" option signs in to a server the user already has an account with (address, username and password). It is not needed for this review, which is why no credentials are supplied.

APP TRANSPORT SECURITY

This build no longer sets NSAllowsArbitraryLoads. It sets only NSAllowsLocalNetworking (home servers on the user's own network) and NSAllowsArbitraryLoadsForMedia (AirPlay of a plain-HTTP stream the user added). Video playback, playlist downloads and artwork do not go through the URL loading system: libmpv and the app's networking layer open their own connections, so ATS does not apply to them. The app uses HTTPS whenever the user's source offers it.

PRIVACY

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting. Watch history and settings stay on the device. Optional sync is available only to a server the user runs themselves; it is off by default. Privacy policy: https://dawnplayer.com/privacy.html

CONTENT AND RIGHTS

Dawn Player does not host, sell, index or recommend content and has no relationship with any content provider. The add-source screen states that the app includes no content and that users must only add sources they have the right to access. Terms: https://dawnplayer.com/terms.html

Home can show lists of well-known titles (from Wikipedia page-view statistics, and from TMDB only with the user's own TMDB key), shown only where a title already exists in the user's own library.
